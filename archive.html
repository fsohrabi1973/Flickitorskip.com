How to read the verdicts

  • Flick it 4 stars and up. Drop what you're doing and go. This is one to see in a theater, on the big screen.
  • Worth it 3½ stars. A good movie that's worth the ticket. It has a few flaws, but you'll be glad you went.
  • Maybe 2½ to 3 stars. Hit and miss. Go if the genre or the cast is your thing, otherwise wait for streaming.
  • Skip it 2 stars or less. Save your money and your evening. Not worth a trip to the theater.
